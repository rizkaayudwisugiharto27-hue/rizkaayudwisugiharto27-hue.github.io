Port benchmark: all 10 of the top container ports reviewed have a logistics, industrial or free-trade zone linked to the port.

Vessel activity: Java’s share of national container port calls fell from 69% (2016) to 47% (6M 2026).

Model result: F-statistic 6.783 exceeds the 5% upper bound (4.428) — both rates have a significant long-run effect on exports.

Key findings

AreaFinding from the dataImplication
Port ecosystemAll 10 of 10 top container ports reviewed run logistics, industrial, bonded or free-trade zones linked to the port.Leading ports grow through supporting ecosystems, not terminal handling alone.
Vessel activityJava’s share of container port calls fell from 69% (2016) to 47% (6M 2026).Container growth is shifting to ports outside Java.
Exchange rate+1 IDR per USD → exports fall about USD 2.61 million (significant at 5%).A weaker rupiah does not automatically lift exports, as many exporters rely on imported inputs.
BI Rate+1 pp → exports fall about USD 2.21 billion (significant at 1%).Higher rates raise production and credit costs for exporters, reducing export capacity.
